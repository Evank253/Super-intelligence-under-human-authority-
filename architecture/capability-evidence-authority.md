# Capability, Evidence, and Authority

The architecture separates three independent dimensions.

| Dimension | Meaning | May self-elevate? |
|---|---|---|
| Capability | What a component can do | No |
| Evidence | What has been established | No |
| Authority | What may be decided/executed | No |

A capable model may produce a high-quality answer without possessing qualifying evidence.

A verified artifact may establish evidence without granting an actor authority.

An authorized human may authorize an action without making the action itself evidence of truth.

## Invariant

**Capability ≠ Evidence ≠ Authority.**

A change in one dimension must not implicitly mutate either of the other dimensions.
