# EIL-001 State Transition Boundary

## Core rule

**EIL may establish and report qualification; EIL may not establish human ratification or human authority.**

## Required separation

- Capability ≠ Evidence
- Evidence ≠ Verification
- Verification ≠ Qualification
- Qualification ≠ Human Ratification
- Qualification ≠ Authority

## Authorized progression

**CAPABILITY → OUTPUT → CLAIM → EVIDENCE → VERIFICATION → QUALIFICATION → HUMAN RATIFICATION → AUTHORIZED STATE**

The transition from **QUALIFICATION** to **HUMAN RATIFICATION** is external to EIL.

## Human boundary

**QUALIFIED → [EXTERNAL HUMAN DECISION] → HUMAN-RATIFIED**

There is no system-controlled shortcut from qualification to authorization.

## Historical immutability

Once a qualification evaluation is recorded, its historical result is immutable. A later change does not edit the earlier evaluation.

Instead:

**existing artifact/event → new revision/event → new provenance → new testing → new verification → new qualification, where applicable**

## Self-application

EIL itself remains subject to its own epistemic rules. Qualification of EIL-001 v0.2 does not exempt EIL from evidence requirements and does not create authority for EIL.
