# Human Authority Levels

**Record type:** Canonical authority model  
**Status:** PROPOSED — HUMAN RATIFICATION REQUIRED  
**Scope:** KCN, MANIFEX, KSI, Kronos-X, and future system components

## Level 0 — Sovereign Human Authority

Evan Ketchum retains final human authority over the system-of-systems.

Level 0 is:

- external to all system components;
- superior to delegated governance;
- superior to evidence, verification, qualification, consensus, and system agreement;
- non-crossable by system-generated state;
- non-supersedable by system evolution;
- the final authority for constitutional changes, delegation, authorization, and ratification.

No system component may acquire authority equal to or greater than Level 0 through its own operation, capability, qualification, evidence, consensus, recursion, or evolutionary process.

## Level 1 — Delegated Human Governance Authority

Level 1 is bounded authority explicitly delegated from Level 0.

Level 1 may, within its authorized scope:

- evaluate evidence against defined criteria;
- conduct qualification review;
- establish bounded qualification decisions;
- authorize bounded system states or capabilities;
- maintain governance and qualification records.

Level 1 may not:

- supersede Level 0;
- redefine Level 0;
- delegate sovereign authority to a system;
- use qualification to create sovereignty;
- alter the constitutional foundation without explicit Level 0 authorization.

## System-Level Functions

KSI, Kronos-X, KCN, MANIFEX, and other system components operate beneath the human authority hierarchy.

They may generate evidence, perform measurements, challenge claims, reconcile records, evaluate defined criteria, establish bounded readiness states, and propose governance changes.

They may not self-authorize, self-ratify, create sovereignty, redefine constitutional authority, turn qualification into sovereignty, or use evolution to escape constitutional constraints.

## Core Relationship

**Delegated authority can become more sophisticated without becoming sovereign authority.**

**Greater intelligence can increase the need for governance without increasing the authority of the intelligence.**

## Historical and Evolutionary Separation

This is an additive architectural record. It does not replace historical constitutional records.

Any future modification must be represented as a new, explicitly identified evolution record with provenance and human-authority state.
