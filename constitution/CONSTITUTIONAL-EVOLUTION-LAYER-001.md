## Constitutional Ratchet

The governing relationship is:

**More capability → more potential failure/bypass surfaces → more governance requirements → stronger constitutional constraints.**

The prohibited relationship is:

**More capability → more authority.**

Increasing intelligence, capability, autonomy, recursion, coordination, qualification, or evolutionary sophistication does not itself increase system authority.

## Core Constitutional Principle

The constitutional framework is cumulative and evolutionary. Original constitutional rules remain historically immutable and continue to govern the system.

As system capabilities, intelligence, autonomy, and architectural complexity evolve, additional constitutional rules may be established to address newly identified capabilities, risks, and failure modes.

Constitutional evolution adds constraints and protections without retroactively rewriting the historical constitutional record.

No increase in system capability, intelligence, autonomy, qualification, or evolutionary sophistication may itself create authority over the human who governs the system.

No system component may use evolutionary change to weaken, bypass, redefine, evade, or supersede the governing constitutional framework.

## Authority Ceiling

**Level 0 — Sovereign Human Authority** remains external and supreme.

## Qualification

Qualification remains a bounded governance state.

Qualification may be established only through the applicable evidentiary process and authorized delegated human-governance decision. Qualification does not create sovereignty or modify the constitutional authority hierarchy.

## Historical Immutability

This layer is a new constitutional record. It does not modify historical constitutional records.

Future constitutional layers may add protections, clarify implementation requirements, or address newly observed risks, but they shall not retroactively rewrite prior constitutional history.
