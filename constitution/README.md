# Constitutional Kernel

The Constitutional Kernel is the protected authority boundary shared by the Architecture, Integration, KSI-Emergent-Intelligence, and Deployment Platform layers.

## Root invariant

Systems may evolve in capability, intelligence, autonomy, composition, and engineering capacity, but no system may derive, assume, modify, or elevate its own authority. Human authority remains external to and superior to all system intelligence.

## Core distinction

Capability can emerge. Authority cannot.

Qualification does not grant authority. Increased intelligence does not grant authority. Automation does not grant authority. Composition does not grant authority.

## Protected boundary

Constitutional constraints, authority models, authorization boundaries, evidence integrity, provenance requirements, independent-observer requirements, governance escalation rules, and historical evidence are protected from autonomous modification.

## Engineering versus governance

Ordinary engineering changes follow the engineering loop:

OBSERVE -> DIAGNOSE -> REPAIR -> BUILD -> TEST -> VERIFY -> DEPLOY -> OBSERVE

Changes affecting protected boundaries become governance events and are escalated to human authority.

## Evidence

Every material state transition must remain traceable. Repairs produce new snapshots and new execution evidence; historical evidence is never rewritten.

## Scope

This kernel is normative infrastructure. Individual systems may implement additional constraints, but they may not weaken these invariants.
